Reference articles
------------------

The following publications provide theoretical and applied context for the
design strategies demonstrated by *doetools*.

* **Experimental design in chemistry: A tutorial** - Riccardo Leardi,
  *Analytica Chimica Acta* 652 (2009), 161-172.

  `DOI 10.1016/j.aca.2009.06.015 <https://doi.org/10.1016/j.aca.2009.06.015>`_

* **Experimental Design** - Riccardo Leardi, in *Chemometrics in Food
  Chemistry*, Data Handling in Science and Technology 28 (2013), 9-53.

  `DOI 10.1016/B978-0-444-59528-7.00002-8 <https://doi.org/10.1016/B978-0-444-59528-7.00002-8>`_

* **D-optimal Designs** - Riccardo Leardi, *Encyclopedia of Analytical
  Chemistry* (2018).

  `DOI 10.1002/9780470027318.a9646 <https://doi.org/10.1002/9780470027318.a9646>`_

* **An example of application of a mixture design with constraints to a
  pharmaceutical formulation** - S. Cafaggi, R. Leardi, B. Parodi,
  G. Caviglioli, and G. Bignardi, *Chemometrics and Intelligent Laboratory
  Systems* 65 (2003), 139-147.

  `DOI 10.1016/S0169-7439(02)00045-X <https://doi.org/10.1016/S0169-7439(02)00045-X>`_
